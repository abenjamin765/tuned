# 01 — Brand Foundations

**TUNED** · Microdose in Key.  
Premium music-themed 1mg Delta-9 THC microdose mint brand.

---

## 1. Positioning

| Layer | Statement |
| --- | --- |
| **Category** | Premium Delta-9 THC microdose mint |
| **Dose architecture** | 1mg THC per mint · 20 mints / 20mg total per box |
| **Differentiator** | Music-tempo product system (movements) + editorial packaging + analog-audio EDC ritual |
| **Audience** | Adults 21+ who value precision, design, and intentional ritual over intensity |
| **Promise** | Consistency you can trust—composed, not chaotic |
| **Price posture** | Premium / collectible |

TUNED is not a party brand, a dispensary meme brand, or a wellness clinic. It sits at the intersection of **warm editorial minimalism**, **surgical precision**, and **analog audio everyday-carry**.

---

## 2. Brand essence

**Essence:** Precision with tempo.

**Tagline:** Microdose in Key.

**Meaning of the tagline:** Every mint is a note—measured, in tune, placed deliberately in the bar. “In key” signals dose accuracy and tonal coherence across flavor, terpene, color, and motif.

---

## 3. Brand pillars

1. **Precision** — 1mg is the unit of truth. No vague “a little.” No volume theater.
2. **Composition** — Products are *movements*, not SKUs with random names.
3. **Restraint** — Quiet luxury. White rigid packaging. Deboss + foil. No loud cannabis tropes.
4. **Ritual** — Pocketable matchbox as EDC; optional NFC soft-tone cue; intentional pace.
5. **Responsibility** — Adults 21+. Clear compliance hierarchy. No medical claims.

---

## 4. Brand voice matrix

### Voice IS

| Trait | Definition | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Precise** | Specific numbers, materials, tempos | “1mg Delta-9 THC per mint. 20 mints per box.” |
| **Composed** | Calm syntax; complete sentences; no hype stacks | “A rising phrase for clear mornings.” |
| **Intentional** | Purpose without therapy language | “Designed for focus without excess.” |
| **Editorial** | Magazine-grade brevity; sensory but controlled | “Warm gold. Arched wave. Sweet peppermint.” |
| **Adult** | Assumes literacy and agency | “Follow local law and personal judgment.” |

### Voice NEVER

| Avoid | Why | Instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stoner slang (*lit, blaze, couch-lock, dank*) | Undermines premium posture | Tempo / movement language |
| Medical / therapeutic claims | Compliance + brand integrity | Sensory + ritual framing |
| “Get high” / intoxication flex | Wrong audience & risk | “Measured,” “composed,” “precise” |
| Bro-marketing / meme tone | Breaks editorial aesthetic | Warm minimalism |
| Fear, shame, or moralizing | Alienating | Clear, neutral responsibility lines |
| Exaggerated efficacy | Unprovable & risky | Dose facts + terpene aroma character |

### Voice spectrum (register)

| Context | Register | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage hero | Elevated editorial | Short lines; tagline-led |
| PDP / movement pages | Descriptive, sensory | Flavor, motif, terpenes, color |
| Science | Explanatory, non-clinical | “Product design, not clinical outcomes” |
| FAQ | Plain, direct | Answer first; compliance clear |
| Legal | Formal | Counsel-ready placeholders |
| Social (future) | Same voice, shorter | Still no slang |

### Tone samples

**On-brand**
> ALLEGRO opens the day the way a score opens a movement—brisk, articulate, and deliberate.

**Off-brand**
> Smash an Allegro and get your mind right, bro—this stuff hits different.

## 5. Messaging hierarchy

1. **Brand** — TUNED / Microdose in Key.
2. **Dose proof** — 1mg · 20 / box · 20mg total.
3. **System** — Three movements (Allegro / Andante / Adagio).
4. **Craft** — White rigid slide matchbox · debossed staff · foil.
5. **Proof of care** — Terpene pairing · NFC soft tone · age gate.

---

## 6. Naming system

| Element | Pattern | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product line | Tempo Italian marking | ALLEGRO, ANDANTE, ADAGIO |
| Catalog number | No. 0X | No. 01, No. 02, No. 03 |
| Bundle | Collective musical noun | The Trio |
| Motifs | Musical gesture | Rising staccato, arched wave, descending fermata |

Do not invent fourth movements without updating foundations, visual tokens, and compliance pack.

## 7. Audience psychographics (summary)

- Design- and music-adjacent adults
- Prefer microdose consistency over intensity
- Carry nice objects (pens, headphones, matchbox-scale EDC)
- Suspicious of medicalized cannabis marketing and of frat cannabis marketing alike

---

## 9. Non-negotiables

- Adults **21+** only; age gate before browse.
- **No medical claims** anywhere (site, pack, ads, NFC destination).
- Dose statement always available near product claims.
- Keep cannabis leaf clichés out of primary identity.
- Web Audio previews only—**no copyrighted music**.
